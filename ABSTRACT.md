# Abstract
ColorChord is a browser-based synesthetic instrument that turns painting into music in real time. Each of seven colours is mapped to a degree of a musical scale (Do to Ti), the vertical position of the brush selects the octave, and the speed of a stroke controls both its width and its loudness, so gestures that look energetic also sound energetic. Users can switch between major, pentatonic and minor scales to change the mood of the same painting, replay a finished artwork as a melody, and save it as an image. Built with HTML5 Canvas, Pointer Events and the Web Audio API, it runs on any laptop, tablet or phone with no installation, making it accessible for classrooms, music-education beginners, and people exploring cross-sensory art. ColorChord explores how visual and musical expression can be translated into each other and offers a playful entry point into both colour theory and musical scales.
